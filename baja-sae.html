Baja SAE – Controls Lead

Project Overview

Wolf Pack Racing is a student engineering team at the University of Nevada, Reno that designs and builds a single-seat off-road vehicle for Baja SAE competition. The project gives students hands-on experience with vehicle design, manufacturing, testing, troubleshooting, and competition.

My Role

I served as Controls Lead from May 2024 through September 2025. My main responsibilities included the throttle, braking, and shifting systems of the vehicle.

Design & Development

I worked on the design, installation, and integration of control components while coordinating with other subteams to make sure the systems worked properly with the rest of the vehicle.

Testing & Troubleshooting

The controls systems required repeated testing and adjustment as the vehicle was assembled and driven. I helped diagnose problems with throttle response, braking, and shifting and made adjustments to improve reliability and performance.

Skills Used

Mechanical Design • Controls • Testing • Troubleshooting • Vehicle Systems • Team Collaboration
