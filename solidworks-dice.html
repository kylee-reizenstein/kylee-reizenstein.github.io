SolidWorks Dice Design

Project Overview

The goal of this project was to create a multi-material die in SolidWorks that maintained an even weight distribution despite the different number of holes on each face.

Design Process

I created the die using parametric modeling and added the face features needed to represent the numbered sides. Different materials and dimensions were evaluated throughout the design process.

Mass Property Analysis

SolidWorks mass-property tools were used to evaluate the center of mass, total mass, volume, surface area, and principal moments of inertia.

Design Optimization

Hole placement, face geometry, thickness, and material selection were adjusted through multiple design iterations. These changes were used to improve the mass distribution and reduce potential bias in the final die.
