1978 Yamaha XS400 Restoration

Project Overview

I acquired this 1978 Yamaha XS400 in non-running condition and began restoring it through mechanical diagnostics, electrical troubleshooting, maintenance, and repair.

Fuel & Intake System

The motorcycle uses a dual-carburetor system. I removed and rebuilt the carburetors, cleaned the components, replaced the air filter, and resealed the air filter housing.

Electrical System

Portions of the electrical system were rewired to correct existing issues. The repairs restored functionality to systems including the taillight and gauges.

Current Work

The motorcycle has been returned to running condition. Current work focuses on continued engine tuning, improving reliability, and completing the cosmetic restoration of the motorcycle.

Skills Used

Carburetor Rebuilding • Electrical Troubleshooting • Wiring • Engine Diagnostics • Mechanical Repair • Restoration
